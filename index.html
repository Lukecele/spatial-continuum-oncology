🧬 Theoretical Continuum & Proof Summary

1. Cylindrical Bessel K₀ Transport

Governed by the steady-state Helmholtz equation ∇²C − λ⁻²C = 0. For outward divergent perivascular cords, the physical solution is C(r) = C₀ · K₀(r/λ) / K₀(r₀/λ). With λ ≈ 627.6 μm, small-molecule osimertinib falls below cellular IC₅₀ = 10 nM at rcrit = 165.9 μm, leaving a 34.1 μm vital shell (31.3% cord volume) with sub-therapeutic drug penetrance.

2. Invariant Lead-Time Kinetic Identity

The 5.4-month clinical window between ctDNA molecular detection (10⁷ cells) and radiographic CT relapse (10⁹ cells) is an invariant mathematical identity of exponential volume doubling:

Δt = Ts · log₂(100) = 6.6439 · Ts ≈ 5.46 months

for doubling time Ts = 24.6 days, exactly resolving the post-cessation ADAURA recurrence surge without de novo mutations.

3. The Composite Resistance Index (CRI)
