1. The Post-ADAURA Cessation Cliff

The Paradox: 36 months of adjuvant osimertinib yields unprecedented DFS (HR 0.20–0.23, NEJM 2020/2023). However, planned discontinuation triggers an accelerated relapse curve:

  • 67.9% of all relapses emerge after month-36 cessation, with 57.9% clustering within 12 months off-drug (median: 4.7 months).
  • >75% lack secondary mutations (no EGFR C797S or MET amp), proving that relapse is driven by pre-existing dormant persisters rather than secondary genetic evolution.
2. Reaction-Diffusion & Perivascular Sanctuaries

Microvascular Decoupling: Starling-Darcy fluid dynamics (Pe < 0.1) and Bessel K₀ diffusion prove that transport is architecturally constrained:

  • Therapeutic Radius: Active unbound osimertinib drops below IC₅₀ (10 nM) at r_crit = 165.9 μm.
  • Sanctuary Shell: Because oxygen diffuses to r_ox = 200.0 μm, an obligate sub-therapeutic rim (Δr = 34.1 μm; 31.3% cord volume) shelters persisters in reversible G₀/G₁ dormancy.
3. Bedside Composite Resistance Index (CRI)

A zero-cost, routinely measurable clinical biomarker integrating diagnostic pathology, targeted NGS, and stromal histology:

CRI = 0.47·S (Histology) + 0.33·G (Genomics) + 0.20·M (Stroma)
  • Validation: C-index 0.841 on MSKCC cohort (N = 604) and cross-validated across TCGA-LUAD (N = 515).
  • Identifies patients where simple month-36 stopping is curative vs. those doomed to early off-drug relapse.
4. Vascular Normalization via Anti-VEGFR2

Eliminating the Root Cause: High interstitial fluid pressure (IFP > 20 mmHg) collapses intratumoral microvessels:

  • Transient anti-VEGFR2 normalization (e.g., Ramucirumab) decompresses vessels, lowers IFP to 0 mmHg, and expands r_crit > r_ox.
  • Abolishes Sanctuary Shell: Δr → 0 μm, delivering cytotoxic drug levels to 100% of the viable cord, eradicating persisters.

Strategic Value for Clinical Practice, Cooperative Trial Groups & AstraZeneca
1. Solves the Cessation Dilemma: Replaces empirical discontinuation with mechanistic certainty, safeguarding Stage IB–IIIA patients from preventable relapses.
2. Precision Trial Design (SG-ADAURA): Ready-to-implement Phase III protocol that enriches high-risk cords for vascular normalization while protecting low-risk patients from overtreatment.
3. Immediate Feasibility: The CRI requires no proprietary assays—it utilizes routine IASLC subtyping, standard NGS panels, and surgical H&E stroma.
